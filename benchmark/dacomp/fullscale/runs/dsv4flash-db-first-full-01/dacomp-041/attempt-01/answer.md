# Comprehensive Preservation Risk Analysis for High-Traffic Exhibition Halls

## Executive Summary

This report analyzes **104 exhibition halls** with average daily visitor counts exceeding **900 visitors** (out of 951 total halls in the database). The analysis integrates environmental monitoring data, artifact condition ratings, sensitivity profiles, display case infrastructure, and formal risk assessments to evaluate core preservation risks and propose prioritized conservation measures.

The elevated UV (39.1 μW/cm²) and IR (5.24 W/m², +4.5% higher) levels accelerate fading, embrittlement, and thermal degradation of organic materials.

## 6. Core Preservation Risks

Based on the integrated analysis, the following core preservation risks are identified in order of severity:

### Risk 1: Particulate & Biological Contamination (CRITICAL)
- **Evidence:** PM2.5 elevated 6.3% above low-traffic halls; microbial counts 10.6% higher; 30.8% exceed PM2.5 thresholds; 28.8% exceed microbial thresholds
- **Impact:** Accelerates chemical degradation, surface soiling, biological growth on organic materials
- **Affected Materials:** Paper (16.3%), textiles (8.7%), wood (12.5%)

### Risk 2: Vibration-Induced Structural Stress (CRITICAL)
- **Evidence:** 39.4% of halls exceed 0.3 mm/s² vibration; 35.6% of artifacts have high vibration sensitivity
- **Impact:** Micro-cracking in ceramics (17.3%), jade (21.2%), and stone (12.5%); structural fatigue in bronze (11.5%)
- **Affected Materials:** Ceramic, jade, stone, bronze

### Risk 3: Excessive Light Exposure (HIGH)
- **Evidence:** Average illuminance 122.4 lux; 27.9% exceed 150 lux; 25% of artifacts are light-sensitive
- **Impact:** UV-induced fading, embrittlement of paper and textiles; IR-induced thermal stress
- **Affected Materials:** Paper (16.3%), textiles (8.7%)

### Risk 4: Microclimate Instability (HIGH)
- **Evidence:** 45.2% of halls have RH outside optimal range; 22.1% have temperature fluctuations >1.5°C; 18.3% have poor seal integrity
- **Impact:** Dimensional changes in hygroscopic materials; salt crystallization in stone/ceramic; mold growth
- **Affected Materials:** Wood, paper, textiles, stone

### Risk 5: Elevated CO₂ & TVOC (MODERATE)
- **Evidence:** 34.6% exceed 800 ppm CO₂; 39.4% exceed 300 ppb TVOC
- **Impact:** Accelerated organic acid degradation of carbonates; potential health effects on staff; corrosion of metals
- **Affected Materials:** Bronze, stone, paper

### Risk 6: Deferred Display Case Maintenance (MODERATE)
- **Evidence:** 40.4% filters need replacement; 31.7% silica gel needs replacement; 37.5% maintenance overdue
- **Impact:** Reduced microclimate control effectiveness; increased pollutant ingress; compromised humidity buffering

## 7. Prioritized Conservation & Maintenance Measures

### Priority 1: Immediate Interventions (0–3 months)

**1.1 Upgrade HVAC Filtration Systems**
- Replace filters in all 42 halls with "Replace Now" status
- Install HEPA filters to reduce PM2.5 ingress (target: <15 µg/m³)
- Enhance particulate filtration to reduce microbial spore loads
- *Expected impact:* Reduces PM2.5 exposure by ~40% and microbial counts by ~25%

**1.2 Replace Silica Gel in Display Cases**
- Immediately replace silica gel in 33 cases with "Replace Now" status
- Schedule replacement for 26 cases with "Replace Soon" status within 2 months
- *Expected impact:* Restores RH buffering capacity, stabilizing microclimate fluctuations

**1.3 Address Display Case Seal Integrity**
- Repair seals in 19 cases with poor integrity
- Verify airtightness to achieve >97% target
- *Expected impact:* Reduces leak rate, improves pollutant and moisture control

**1.4 Implement Vibration Damping**
- Install vibration isolation mounts for display cases in high-traffic areas
- Prioritize 37 halls with high vibration sensitivity artifacts
- *Expected impact:* Reduces vibration transmission by 50-70%

### Priority 2: Short-Term Measures (3–6 months)

**2.1 Enhanced Lighting Control**
- Reduce illuminance on paper and textile artifacts to ≤50 lux
- Install UV-filtering films on all light sources (target: <10 μW/lumen)
- Implement automated dimming systems responsive to ambient light
- *Expected impact:* Reduces fading rate by 60-80% for photosensitive materials

**2.2 Optimize HVAC Set Points**
- Calibrate temperature targets to 18-20°C (±0.5°C daily fluctuation)
- Calibrate RH targets to 48-52% (±2% daily fluctuation)
- Address 24 halls with out-of-range temperature fluctuations
- *Expected impact:* Reduces dimensional stress on hygroscopic materials

**2.3 Conduct Overdue Maintenance**
- Complete maintenance for 39 cases with overdue status
- Perform preventive maintenance on 36 cases with due status
- *Expected impact:* Restores full microclimate control functionality

### Priority 3: Medium-Term Measures (6–12 months)

**3.1 Implement Continuous Environmental Monitoring**
- Deploy real-time CO₂, PM2.5, and TVOC sensors in all 104 high-traffic halls
- Establish automated alerts for threshold exceedances
- Integrate with building management system for responsive HVAC control
- *Expected impact:* Enables proactive environmental management

**3.2 Visitor Flow Management**
- Implement timed entry and capacity management systems
- Reduce peak crowding in 41 high-risk halls
- Consider visitor routing to distribute foot traffic
- *Expected impact:* Reduces CO₂, vibration, and particulate resuspension by 15-20%

**3.3 Enhanced Cleaning Protocols**
- Implement HEPA vacuum cleaning schedules for display cases
- Increase cleaning frequency in 23 halls with high dust accumulation
- Use anti-static cleaning methods to reduce particulate re-deposition
- *Expected impact:* Reduces dust accumulation and microbial load

### Priority 4: Long-Term Strategic Measures (12–24 months)

**4.1 Comprehensive Conservation Treatment**
- Prioritize treatment for 25 relics with urgent priority and rapid deterioration
- Allocate conservation budget proportional to insurance values (avg $518,891 per relic)
- Implement phased treatment schedule based on material type and condition severity

**4.2 Upgrade Display Case Infrastructure**
- Replace aging display cases with improved airtightness (>98%)
- Upgrade to microclimate-controlled cases with active RH buffering
- Install inert gas systems for highly sensitive artifacts
- *Expected impact:* Long-term stabilization of artifact preservation environment

**4.3 Staff Training & Emergency Preparedness**
- Conduct conservation awareness training for all hall staff
- Update emergency response plans for 41 high-risk halls
- Establish rapid response protocols for environmental excursions
- *Expected impact:* Improved prevention and response to preservation emergencies

## 8. Conclusions

The analysis of 104 high-traffic exhibition halls (>900 daily visitors) reveals a **convergence of multiple risk factors** that collectively threaten cultural relic preservation. The most critical findings are:

1. **Biological and particulate contamination** (+10.6% microbial, +6.3% PM2.5) is the most pervasive environmental risk, directly linked to visitor density.

2. **Vibration stress** affects 39.4% of halls and 35.6% of vibration-sensitive artifacts, creating structural risks for ceramics, jade, and stone.

3. **Light exposure** (average 122.4 lux) is excessive for the 25% of artifacts that are paper or textile, accelerating irreversible fading and embrittlement.

4. **Display case infrastructure** is compromised: 40.4% of filters and 31.7% of silica gel need replacement, while 37.5% of cases have overdue maintenance.

5. **Formal risk assessment** classifies 39.4% of these halls as "High Risk," with 50% of their artifacts requiring urgent or high-priority treatment.

The proposed conservation measures are prioritized to address the most immediate threats first (filtration, seal integrity, vibration damping), followed by environmental optimization (lighting, HVAC), and culminating in long-term infrastructure upgrades and conservation treatment. This tiered approach ensures that the most critical preservation risks in high-traffic environments are addressed promptly while building toward sustainable long-term protection of the cultural heritage assets.
