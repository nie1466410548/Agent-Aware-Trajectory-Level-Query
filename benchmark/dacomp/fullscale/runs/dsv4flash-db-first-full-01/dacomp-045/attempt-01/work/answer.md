# High-Value Users vs Regular Users: Comparative Analysis Report

## Executive Summary

This report analyzes behavioral differences between **High-Value users** (Diamond & Platinum membership, n=233) and **Regular users** (n=132) across product category/brand preferences in Search, Favorites, and Browsing, as well as Search active time patterns. The analysis provides data-driven recommendations for tiered operations strategies and personalized product recommendations.

## 8. Targeted Tiered Operations Strategies

### 8.1 For High-Value Users (Diamond & Platinum)

**Profile**: 50+ years old, highly educated (Master's/PhD), mid-to-high income, research-oriented buyers, weekend-active, less price-sensitive.

**Strategies**:

1. **Premium Home & Living + Sports Curation**
   - Prioritize recommendations for **Home & Living** and **Sports & Outdoors** categories
   - Cross-sell premium brands (BYD, Haier, Tsingtao) within these categories
   - Create "Premium Lifestyle" collection bundles

2. **Weekend Exclusive Events**
   - Schedule flash sales and new-arrival previews on **Saturday and Sunday** (peak activity days)
   - Launch "Weekend Premium Picks" campaigns with high-ticket items

3. **Deep-Content Product Pages**
   - Invest in **detailed specifications, zoomable images, 360° views, and professional reviews**
   - High-Value users check specs (+4.4pp) and reviews (+5.2pp) more than Regular users
   - Provide comparison tools and expert reviews

4. **Afternoon/Night Trigger Campaigns**
   - Push notifications around **15:00 and 23:00** (peak search hours)
   - Offer limited-time evening deals

5. **Higher Price Point Recommendations**
   - Surface products in the **¥2,500+ range** as primary recommendations
   - Focus on quality/value messaging rather than discount/promotion

### 8.2 For Regular Users

**Profile**: 36-50 years old, more varied education, evening-active, more exploratory searchers, price-conscious.

**Strategies**:

1. **Digital 3C + Food & Beverage Focus**
   - Prioritize **Digital 3C** (phones, laptops, accessories) and **Food & Beverages**
   - Recommend budget-friendly alternatives and popular brands (Kweichow Moutai, Yunnan Baiyao)

2. **Evening Prime Time Engagement (18:00-21:00)**
   - Schedule main promotional pushes during **evening hours**
   - "Evening Flash Deals" with countdown timers

3. **Guided Discovery & Search Assistance**
   - Regular users view 14% more results and click 15% more — provide better **search filters, sorting, and recommendation carousels**
   - Offer "Popular Searches" and trending product lists
   - Improve autocomplete suggestions (already used at 54.7%)

4. **Value-Driven Messaging**
   - Regular users favorited products average ¥2,486 vs ¥2,570 for High-Value
   - Emphasize **discounts, bundle deals, and free shipping**
   - Show "savings" prominently

5. **Midweek Engagement**
   - Regular users are more evenly active across weekdays — use **Monday-Thursday** for drip campaigns and educational content

### 8.3 Optimized Product Recommendation Plan

| Recommendation Dimension | High-Value Users | Regular Users |
|---|---|---|
| **Primary Categories** | Home & Living, Sports & Outdoors | Digital 3C, Food & Beverages |
| **Secondary Categories** | Clothing, beauty & skincare | Home Appliances, Sports |

## 9. Limitations

1. **Sample Size**: With 233 High-Value and 132 Regular users, the statistical power for detecting small differences is limited. Chi-square tests did not reach significance (p>0.05), though consistent directional patterns emerged.
2. **Data Quality**: The product table contains seemingly synthetic mappings (e.g., "Gree Air Conditioner" under "Home & Living" with brand "Yunnan Baiyao"), so category-brand relationships may not be realistic.
3. **Search Keyword Mapping**: Search keywords were manually mapped to categories for analysis; some keywords (e.g., "toothbrush" → beauty & skincare) may be debatable.
4. **Gold Group Excluded**: Gold members (n=126) were treated as a separate intermediate group and not directly compared. Their inclusion could reveal graduations in behavior.
5. **Cross-Sectional Data**: The analysis is based on historical behavior records without temporal tracking of membership changes. Causal inferences cannot be drawn.

---

## 10. Conclusion

High-Value users (Diamond/Platinum) and Regular users exhibit **meaningful descriptive differences** in category preferences, brand affinities, search timing, and engagement depth. While not all differences reach statistical significance, the consistent patterns across three behavioral dimensions (favorites, browsing, search) support a **tiered operations strategy**:

- **High-Value**: Premium, research-oriented, weekend-active — focus on Home & Living, Sports, deep content, and high-ticket items
- **Regular**: Discovery-oriented, evening-active, price-conscious — focus on Digital 3C, Food & Beverages, guided search, and value promotions

Implementing the differentiated recommendation plan could enhance user stickiness by aligning content delivery with each segment's natural preferences and active times, while the premium positioning for High-Value users supports revenue growth through higher average order values.
