Among the North American stock indices (those tracking the New York Stock Exchange, NASDAQ, and Toronto Stock Exchange per index_info), ALL THREE had more up days than down days in 2018:

1. NYA — NYSE Composite (New York Stock Exchange): 134 up days vs. 117 down days
2. IXIC — NASDAQ Composite (NASDAQ): 134 up days vs. 116 down days (1 unchanged)
3. GSPTSE — S&P/TSX Composite (Toronto Stock Exchange): 130 up days vs. 121 down days

Definition used: an "up day" is a trading day whose Close is higher than the previous trading day's Close (the first 2018 trading day is compared against the last 2017 close); a "down day" has Close lower than the previous close. Each index had 251 trading days in 2018. Date strings in index_trade use mixed formats and were parsed accordingly before ordering.
