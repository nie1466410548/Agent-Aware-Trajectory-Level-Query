QUESTION: If an investor had made regular monthly investments in all indices since 2000, which 5 indices would have produced the highest overall returns, and what countries do they belong to?

METHODOLOGY:
- Simulated dollar-cost averaging: a fixed $1 (USD) invested at the first trading day of every month from January 2000 onward (or from the first date data is available for indices that start later), using the CloseUSD prices from index_trade.
- Shares bought each month = 1 / CloseUSD; total portfolio value = accumulated shares x final CloseUSD (last available date, ~end of May/early June 2021). Overall return = final value / total invested - 1.
- Countries/exchanges are identified from the index symbols and the exchange/currency metadata in index_info (NASDAQ/USD, National Stock Exchange of India/INR, Shenzhen Stock Exchange/CNY, Frankfurt Stock Exchange/EUR, Taiwan Stock Exchange/TWD).

ANSWER - TOP 5 INDICES BY OVERALL RETURN ON MONTHLY INVESTMENTS SINCE 2000:

1. IXIC - NASDAQ Composite - United States (NASDAQ)
   Return: ~383% ($257 invested grew to ~$1,241; monthly investing Jan 2000 - May 2021)

2. NSEI - NIFTY 50 - India (National Stock Exchange of India)
   Return: ~136% ($165 invested grew to ~$389; data available from Sep 2007 - May 2021)

3. 399001.SZ - Shenzhen Component Index - China (Shenzhen Stock Exchange)
   Return: ~135% ($258 invested grew to ~$606; Jan 2000 - Jun 2021)

4. GDAXI - DAX - Germany (Frankfurt Stock Exchange)
   Return: ~135% ($257 invested grew to ~$603; Jan 2000 - May 2021)

5. TWII - Taiwan Capitalization Weighted Stock Index (TAIEX) - Taiwan (Taiwan Stock Exchange)
   Return: ~130% ($257 invested grew to ~$591; Jan 2000 - May 2021)

(For reference, the next indices were N225/Japan ~117%, NYA/USA ~101%, GSPTSE/Canada ~73%, 000001.SS/China ~64%, N100/Euronext ~61%, HSI/Hong Kong ~60%, SSMI/Switzerland ~59%, J203.JO/South Africa ~38%.)
