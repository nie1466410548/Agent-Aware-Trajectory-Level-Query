TASK: CPC technology areas in Germany with the highest exponential moving average (smoothing factor 0.1) of yearly patent filings, for patents granted in the second half of 2019 (July 1 – December 31, 2019), at CPC level 4.

METHOD:
- Selected patents whose publication number is German (DE-...) and whose grant date falls in H2 2019: 34 patents (out of 68 DE patents granted in 2019; the other 34 were granted Jan–Jun 2019).
- Extracted each patent's distinct CPC codes and mapped them to the level-4 CPC group (class, 3-character symbol, per cpc_definition where level = 4, e.g. "A01").
- Counted filings per level-4 group per filing year and computed the EMA over consecutive years (alpha = 0.1, recursive EMA: ema_t = 0.1*count_t + 0.9*ema_{t-1}, gap years count as 0).
- The "best year" for each group is the year with the highest EMA value; groups are ranked by that peak EMA.
