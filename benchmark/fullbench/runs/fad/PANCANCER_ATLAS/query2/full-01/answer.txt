Among BRCA (breast invasive carcinoma) patients in the PanCancer Atlas who are alive (936 patients total), the top three histological types with the highest percentage of CDH1 gene mutations are:

1. Infiltrating Lobular Carcinoma — 50.56% (90 of 178 alive patients carry a CDH1 mutation)
2. Mixed Histology (please specify) — 16.67% (4 of 24 alive patients)
3. Other, specify — 8.33% (3 of 36 alive patients)

(For comparison, Infiltrating Ductal Carcinoma, the most common type, has only 2.09% — 14 of 671 alive patients.)

Method: Alive BRCA patients were identified from clinical_info (Patient_description indicating breast invasive carcinoma and vital status Alive; cross-validated with days_to_death = '[Not Applicable]'), and CDH1 mutation carriers were taken as distinct ParticipantBarcodes in Mutation_Data with Hugo_Symbol = 'CDH1'. Percentages were computed per histological_type as mutated patients / total alive patients of that type.
