Average log10-transformed IGF2 expression for LGG (Brain Lower Grade Glioma) patients, by histological type
(Only patients with valid, positive IGF2 normalized_count values and histology annotations not enclosed in square brackets were included.)

Astrocytoma: 2.5700 (n=10 patients)
Oligoastrocytoma: 2.7126 (n=5 patients)
Oligodendroglioma: 2.6814 (n=8 patients)

Method: LGG patients (513) were identified in clinical_info via Patient_description mentioning "Lower Grade Glioma"; full TCGA barcodes were extracted from Patient_description; histological_type values were filtered to exclude NULL and bracket-enclosed annotations (none existed for LGG). IGF2 normalized_count values (non-null, >0) were taken from RNASeq_Expression in the molecular database and inner-joined on patient barcode (23 patients matched). log10 was applied per patient, then averaged within each histology group.
