Among repositories that do not use Python, approximately 13.8% of their README.md files include copyright information.

Methodology:
- The artifacts database contains 204 README.md files (case-insensitive match on paths ending in "readme.md", at root or nested) across 200 repositories with retrievable content.
- These repos were matched against the metadata database's languages table (178 of 200 found). A repo "uses Python" if its language_description mentions "Python"; otherwise it is classified as non-Python.
- A README.md is considered to include copyright information if its content contains the word "copyright" (case-insensitive) or the © symbol.

Results:
- Non-Python repositories with README.md files: 135, containing 138 README.md files.
- README.md files containing copyright information: 19 of 138 → proportion = 19/138 ≈ 0.138 (13.8%).
- At the repository level: 19 of 135 non-Python repos (14.1%) have at least one README.md with copyright information.
- Sensitivity check: if the 22 repos absent from the metadata languages table are also counted as non-Python, the figure is 21/160 ≈ 13.1%.

Answer: about 13.8% (19 out of 138 README.md files from non-Python repositories).
