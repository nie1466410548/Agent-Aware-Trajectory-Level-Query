TASK: Among users who registered on Yelp in 2016, which 5 business categories have received the most total reviews from those users since 2016?

METHOD:
- Identified 168 users in user_database.user whose yelping_since date falls in 2016 (mixed date formats parsed by extracting the 4-digit year).
- Selected reviews from user_database.review written by those users with review date in 2016 or later (150 qualifying reviews; reviews with null user_id excluded).
- Linked review.business_ref ("businessref_N") to businessinfo_database.business.business_id ("businessid_N"); all reviewed businesses matched.
- Business categories were extracted from each business's description text (the category list embedded in the description); all 100 business descriptions were parsed and manually verified.

ANSWER - Top 5 business categories by total reviews from 2016-registered users since 2016:
1. Restaurants - 58 reviews
2. Food - 38 reviews
3. American (New) - 24 reviews
4. Shopping - 20 reviews
5. Breakfast & Brunch - 19 reviews

(Next closest: Automotive and Local Services, 17 each.)
