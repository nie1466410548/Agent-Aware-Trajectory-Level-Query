Answer: 35

Methodology:
1. From user_database.review (DuckDB), parsed all 2,000 review dates (which appear in three mixed formats: "YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM:SS", "DD Mon YYYY, HH:MM", and "Month DD, YYYY at HH:MM AM/PM") and found 67 distinct businesses (business_ref) that received at least one review during calendar year 2018.
2. Mapped review.business_ref ("businessref_N") to business.business_id ("businessid_N") in businessinfo_database.business (MongoDB) by numeric suffix and fetched the attributes of those 67 businesses (all 67 found).
3. A business counts as offering parking if its attributes indicate either:
   - business parking: a BusinessParking dict with at least one of garage/street/validated/lot/valet = True, OR
   - bike parking: BikeParking = "True".
   (Businesses with a BusinessParking dict where all values are False/None, with BusinessParking = "None", or with no attributes were not counted as offering parking.)
Result: 35 of the 67 businesses reviewed in 2018 offered either business parking or bike parking.

Note: under a looser interpretation that merely requires the BusinessParking attribute key to be present (even if all parking types are False), the count would be 40; the stricter and semantically correct reading of "offered parking" yields 35.
