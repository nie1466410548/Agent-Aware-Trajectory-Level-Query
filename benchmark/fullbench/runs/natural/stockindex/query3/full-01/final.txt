TASK: If an investor had made regular monthly investments in all indices since 2000, which 5 indices would have produced the highest overall returns, and what countries do they belong to?

METHODOLOGY:
- Used indextrade_database (DuckDB) daily prices, CloseUSD (USD-converted closes) for cross-country comparability.
- Simulated dollar-cost averaging: invest $1 at the first trading day of every month from January 2000 (or the index's first available date after 2000) through each index's last available date (~May/June 2021), accumulating units.
- Overall return = (final portfolio value - total invested) / total invested.
- Countries determined by matching each index symbol to its exchange in indexinfo_database (index_info table).

RESULTS - TOP 5 INDICES BY OVERALL RETURN:

1. IXIC (NASDAQ Composite) — United States (NASDAQ)
   Return: +382.7% ($257 invested monthly Jan 2000–May 2021 grew to $1,240.52)

2. NSEI (NIFTY 50) — India (National Stock Exchange of India)
   Return: +135.8% ($165 invested monthly Sep 2007–May 2021 grew to $389.14)

3. 399001.SZ (Shenzhen Component) — China (Shenzhen Stock Exchange)
   Return: +134.7% ($258 invested grew to $605.65)

4. GDAXI (DAX) — Germany (Frankfurt Stock Exchange)
   Return: +134.7% ($257 invested grew to $603.19)

5. TWII (Taiwan Weighted) — Taiwan (Taiwan Stock Exchange)
   Return: +129.8% ($257 invested grew to $590.64)

For reference, the remaining indices ranked: N225 (Japan) +116.9%, NYA (United States) +101.5%, GSPTSE (Canada) +73.0%, 000001.SS (China) +64.3%, N100 (Euronext/pan-Europe) +61.3%, HSI (Hong Kong) +60.5%, SSMI (Switzerland) +58.8%, J203.JO (South Africa) +37.5%.

NOTE: Some indices have data starting after 2000 (NSEI from Sep 2007, J203.JO from Feb 2012); for those, monthly investments began at their first available date. The NASDAQ Composite (US) is the clear winner; the remaining top-5 order (India, China-Shenzhen, Germany, Taiwan) is robust because their returns cluster well above the rest.
