Task: Among North American stock indices, which indices had more up days than down days in 2018?

Method:
- North American exchanges from index_info (SQLite): New York Stock Exchange, NASDAQ (USA) and Toronto Stock Exchange (Canada).
- Corresponding indices in index_trade (DuckDB): NYA (NYSE Composite), IXIC (NASDAQ Composite), GSPTSE (S&P/TSX Composite).
- An "up day" = Close > Open; a "down day" = Close < Open. Counted all 2018 trading days for each index.

Results (2018):
- IXIC (NASDAQ Composite): 131 up days vs 120 down days -> MORE UP THAN DOWN
- NYA (NYSE Composite): 125 up days vs 126 down days -> no
- GSPTSE (S&P/TSX Composite): 115 up days vs 135 down days (1 flat day) -> no

Answer: Only IXIC (the NASDAQ Composite index) had more up days than down days in 2018.
