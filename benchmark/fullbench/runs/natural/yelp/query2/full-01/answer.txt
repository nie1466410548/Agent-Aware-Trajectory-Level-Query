ANSWER: Missouri (MO) has the highest number of reviews, and the average rating of businesses in that state is approximately 3.91.

METHOD:
1. Extracted each business's U.S. state from the `description` field of the MongoDB `business` collection (e.g., "in St. Louis, MO,") using a state-abbreviation pattern. All 100 businesses parsed successfully.
2. Summed the `review_count` metadata per state. Missouri (MO) ranked first with 2,243 total reviews across 9 businesses, ahead of Pennsylvania (PA, 2,022) and Florida (FL, 565).
3. Joined the 9 Missouri businesses (businessid_4, 10, 11, 24, 30, 46, 47, 64, 96) to the DuckDB `review` table via the numeric identifier suffix (businessid_N -> businessref_N) and averaged the ratings of all their reviews (213 reviews in the review table).

RESULT:
- State with the most reviews: Missouri (MO), 2,243 total reviews (business metadata review_count).
- Average rating of Missouri businesses: 3.9061 (review-weighted average of ratings across all 213 reviews; simple mean of per-business average ratings is 4.00).
