CHI-SQUARE TEST: Histological Type vs. CDH1 Mutation Status in Female BRCA (PanCancer Atlas)

METHOD
- Cohort: 1,075 female BRCA (Breast invasive carcinoma) patients identified from the clinical database
  (barcode = 'TCGA-' + tissue_source_site + '-' + patient_id; gender extracted from Patient_description).
- Restricted to patients with known histological types: 1,074 patients (1 null excluded).
- CDH1 mutation presence: patients with at least one reliable CDH1 mutation entry
  (Hugo_Symbol = 'CDH1' AND FILTER = 'PASS') in Mutation_Data (247 distinct participants across all cancers;
  99 overlapped the female BRCA cohort).
- Histological categories with marginal totals <= 10 excluded:
  Metaplastic Carcinoma (8), Medullary Carcinoma (6), Infiltrating Carcinoma NOS (1).

CONTINGENCY TABLE (N = 1,059; mutated / not mutated)
- Infiltrating Ductal Carcinoma:    9  / 757  (total 766)
- Infiltrating Lobular Carcinoma:  83  / 118  (total 201)
- Other, specify:                   3  / 42   (total 45)
- Mixed Histology (please specify): 4  / 26   (total 30)
- Mucinous Carcinoma:               0  / 17   (total 17)

RESULT
- Chi-square statistic: 305.12
- Degrees of freedom: 4
- p-value: ~8.5e-65

INTERPRETATION
There is a highly significant association between histological type and CDH1 mutation status in female
BRCA patients. CDH1 mutations are strongly enriched in Infiltrating Lobular Carcinoma (83/201, ~41.3%)
compared with Infiltrating Ductal Carcinoma (9/766, ~1.2%) and other histologies — consistent with the
known biology of lobular breast cancer, which is characterized by CDH1 (E-cadherin) loss.
