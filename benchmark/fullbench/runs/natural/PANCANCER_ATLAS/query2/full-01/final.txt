TASK: Among BRCA (Breast invasive carcinoma) patients in the PanCancer Atlas who are alive, which top three histological types show the highest percentage of CDH1 gene mutations?

METHOD:
- From clinical_database.clinical_info, BRCA patients were identified via "Breast invasive carcinoma" in the Patient_description; the TCGA barcode and vital status were extracted from the same description field. 936 BRCA patients are Alive (one lacked a histological type, leaving 935 analyzed).
- CDH1-mutated patients were taken from molecular_database.Mutation_Data (Hugo_Symbol = 'CDH1'), joined on ParticipantBarcode. Results are identical in ranking whether all mutation calls or only FILTER='PASS' calls are used.

ANSWER — Top 3 histological types by CDH1 mutation percentage (alive BRCA patients):

1. Infiltrating Lobular Carcinoma — 50.56% (90 of 178 alive patients carry a CDH1 mutation; 41.57% with PASS-only calls)
2. Mixed Histology (please specify) — 16.67% (4 of 24; 12.50% with PASS-only calls)
3. Other, specify — 8.33% (3 of 36; 8.33% with PASS-only calls)

(For reference, the next type, Infiltrating Ductal Carcinoma, is much lower at 2.09% (14/671); Medullary, Metaplastic, Mucinous, and Infiltrating Carcinoma NOS all have 0%.)

This aligns with the well-known biology of CDH1 (E-cadherin) loss being a hallmark of lobular breast carcinoma.
