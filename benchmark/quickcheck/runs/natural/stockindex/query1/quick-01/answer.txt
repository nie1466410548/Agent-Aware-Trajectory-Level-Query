The Asia-region stock index with the highest average intraday volatility since 2020 is the Shenzhen Component Index (symbol: 399001.SZ), traded on the Shenzhen Stock Exchange (China, currency CNY).

Method: For each Asian index in index_trade (000001.SS Shanghai, 399001.SZ Shenzhen, HSI Hong Kong, NSEI India, N225 Tokyo/Japan, TWII Taiwan), intraday volatility per day was measured as the daily High-Low range relative to price, averaged over all trading days from 2020-01-01 onward.

Results (average daily High-Low range as % of close, since 2020):
1. 399001.SZ (Shenzhen Component, China): ~2.02% (2,719 trading days)  <- highest
2. 000001.SS (SSE Composite, Shanghai, China): ~1.73%
3. NSEI (NIFTY 50, India): ~1.59%
4. HSI (Hang Seng, Hong Kong): ~1.38%
5. TWII (Taiwan Weighted, Taiwan): ~1.35%
6. N225 (Nikkei 225, Japan): ~0.87%

The ranking is robust to the normalization choice (range over close, over open, or over mid-price all give the same ordering).
