TASK: Among North American stock indices, which indices had more up days than down days in 2018?

ANSWER: Only the NASDAQ Composite (index symbol: IXIC).

REASONING:
- The index_info database lists 14 exchanges; the North American ones are the New York Stock Exchange (USD), NASDAQ (USD), and Toronto Stock Exchange (CAD).
- The corresponding indices in the index_trade database are: NYA (NYSE Composite), IXIC (NASDAQ Composite), and GSPTSE (S&P/TSX Composite).
- An "up day" is a trading day where Close > Open, and a "down day" where Close < Open. All 2018 trading days were analyzed (mixed date formats parsed and verified to fall in year 2018; 251 trading days each).

Results for 2018:
- IXIC (NASDAQ Composite): 131 up days vs 120 down days -> MORE UP DAYS THAN DOWN DAYS
- NYA (NYSE Composite): 125 up days vs 126 down days -> no
- GSPTSE (S&P/TSX Composite, Toronto): 115 up days vs 135 down days (1 flat day) -> no

Therefore, the only North American stock index with more up days than down days in 2018 was the NASDAQ Composite (IXIC).
