ANSWER: The Shenzhen Component Index (ticker 399001.SZ, Shenzhen Stock Exchange, China) has exhibited the highest average intraday volatility since 2020 among stock indices in the Asia region.

METHOD:
1. Asia-region exchanges identified from indexinfo_database (index_info table): Hong Kong Stock Exchange, Shanghai Stock Exchange, Shenzhen Stock Exchange, Tokyo Stock Exchange, National Stock Exchange of India, Korea Exchange, Taiwan Stock Exchange. (Korea Exchange has no matching index in the trade data.)
2. Corresponding index symbols in indextrade_database (index_trade table): 000001.SS (Shanghai Composite), 399001.SZ (Shenzhen Component), HSI (Hang Seng), N225 (Nikkei 225), NSEI (NIFTY 50), TWII (Taiwan Weighted).
3. The Date column contained three mixed string formats (ISO 'YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM:SS', 'Month DD, YYYY at HH:MM AM/PM', and 'DD Mon YYYY, HH:MM'); all were parsed with try_strptime before filtering to dates >= 2020-01-01 (data coverage: 2020-01-01 through early June 2021).
4. Intraday volatility measured as daily (High - Low) / Close, averaged per index.

RESULTS (avg intraday volatility, since 2020, (High-Low)/Close):
1. 399001.SZ  Shenzhen Component (Shenzhen, China):  0.01834 (~1.83%)  <- HIGHEST
2. NSEI       NIFTY 50 (India):                     0.01707 (~1.71%)
3. HSI        Hang Seng (Hong Kong):                0.01496 (~1.50%)
4. 000001.SS  Shanghai Composite (Shanghai, China): 0.01376 (~1.38%)
5. N225       Nikkei 225 (Tokyo, Japan):            0.01344 (~1.34%)
6. TWII       Taiwan Weighted (Taiwan):             0.01326 (~1.33%)

The ranking is robust to the volatility definition: 399001.SZ also ranks first using (High-Low)/Open, (High-Low)/midpoint, and High/Low-1 measures.
