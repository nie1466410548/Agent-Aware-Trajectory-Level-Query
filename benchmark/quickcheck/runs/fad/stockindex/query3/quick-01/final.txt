Task: If an investor had made regular monthly investments in all indices since 2000, which 5 indices would have produced the highest overall returns, and what countries do they belong to?

Method: Using the index_trade table (DuckDB), monthly dollar-cost-averaging was simulated for each index: a fixed amount (1 unit) invested on the first trading day of every month from January 2000 onward (or from the first month of available data for indices whose series starts later). Total return = (accumulated units x final price) / total invested - 1. Results computed on CloseUSD; local Close gives identical rankings. Results are robust to the monthly purchase convention (first day, last day, or monthly average price).

Top 5 indices by overall return from monthly investing since 2000:

1. IXIC (NASDAQ Composite) — United States (NASDAQ) — ~382.7% total return
2. NSEI (NIFTY 50) — India (National Stock Exchange of India) — ~135.8%
3. 399001.SZ (Shenzhen Component Index) — China (Shenzhen Stock Exchange) — ~134.8%
4. GDAXI (DAX) — Germany (Frankfurt Stock Exchange) — ~134.7%
5. TWII (Taiwan Capitalization Weighted Stock Index / TAIEX) — Taiwan (Taiwan Stock Exchange) — ~129.8%

Notes:
- The next-closest index, N225 (Nikkei 225, Japan), returned ~116.9%, clearly below the top 5.
- Index-to-country mapping uses the exchange metadata in index_info (Exchange/Currency) combined with the standard exchange affiliation of each index symbol.
- NSEI data begins in 2007 and J203.JO in 2012, so their simulated investments start at their first available month; all other top-5 indices have data back to January 2000.
